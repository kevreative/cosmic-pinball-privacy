Privacy Policy for Cosmic Pinball

Last updated: August 2, 2026

Overview

Cosmic Pinball ("the App") is a free, ad-supported mobile game developed by Kevreative. This policy explains how data is handled when you use the App.

Data We Collect

We do not directly collect any personal information. The App only stores your high score locally on your device. This data never leaves your device.

Third-Party Services – Google AdMob

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect the following data on our behalf:

  • Advertising identifier (IDFA), if you grant permission
  • Device information (model, operating system version)
  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

This data is collected and processed by Google in accordance with their Privacy Policy.

App Tracking Transparency

On iOS, the App will request your permission before accessing your advertising identifier. You may deny this request and the App will continue to function normally with non-personalized ads.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Your Rights

Since we do not collect personal data directly, there is no personal data for us to provide, modify, or delete. For data collected by Google AdMob, please refer to Google's privacy controls at myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy.

Contact
